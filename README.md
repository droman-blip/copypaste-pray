# 🚀 Orbital Mission

![Orbital Mission Banner](./WhatsApp%20Image%202026-11-15%20at%2014.30.58.jpeg)

**Orbital Mission** is a 3D cooperative space mission simulator (1–4 players) built around real **NASA Systems Engineering** methodologies. Step into an industrial launch complex, collaborate across specialized aerospace departments, and engineer a complete space mission from initial concept to liftoff.

## ✨ Key Features

- **1–4 Players (Solo & Co-op):** Play single-player or team up with friends in real time.
- **NASA 6-Phase Lifecycle (72 Missions):** Progress through official aerospace engineering gates (*Identify Need & Objectives* through *Mission Operations*), balancing **Mass (kg)**, **Budget ($M)**, **Science Yield**, and **Mission Risk (%)**.
- **4 Specialized Roles:**
  - 🟠 **Mechanic:** Rocket propulsion, structural integrity, cryogenic tanks, and thermal heat shields.
  - 🔵 **Engineer:** Avionics, power budgets, radar comms, and orbital trajectory mechanics.
  - 🟢 **Scientist:** Science payloads, sensor calibration, material testing, and life support systems.
  - 🟡 **Program Manager:** Global budget allocation, procurement audits, and gate approvals.
- **3 Difficulty Tiers:**
  - **Easy:** Low Earth Orbit (LEO)
  - **Medium:** Lunar Mission (Artemis-class)
  - **Hard:** Mars Expedition
- **Interactive 3D Labs & A.R.I.A. AI Copilot:** Inspect 3D rocket components in exploded view, run live orbital trajectory simulations, stress-test materials with a thermal torch, and consult **A.R.I.A.** on your diegetic Mission Tablet.
